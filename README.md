# Cyclistic_Bike_Share Analysis
This is my capstone Google Data Analytics certificate. An exploratory data analysis of Cyclistic’s bike-share data to identify rider behavior in 2024 using patterns, usage trends, and strategic opportunities for improving customer retention. Conducted data cleaning, analysis, and visualization on Cyclistic’s bike-share data using Excel and Tableau to generate actionable business recommendations.

# 🎯 Project Objectives

# 🛠️ Tools Used
- Excel→ for data cleaning, querying, grouping, and calculating net profit
- Tableau → for creating interactive dashboards and visualizing insights
- Micrsoft Power Point → for creating a presentation with  business recommendation
# 🔎 Analysis Focus
 Cyclistic marketing strategy focus on broad consumer appeal with three pricing options:  
 1) Singgle ride  
 2) Full day pass  
 3) Annual membership  
 Financial analysis indicates annual membership are more profitable  

 Future Growth Strategy:  
 1. Maximazing Cyclistic Member for future growth  
 2. Convert Casual Bikers to Cyclistic Member  

# 🪄 Recommendation  
Marketing Team  
1. Develop strategies to convert casual riders to cyclistic 
members during colder months (e.g., offering indoor 
cycling partnerships, seasonal membership discounts, 
or exclusive winter benefits).  
2. Tailor marketing campaigns to convert casual riders to  
cyclistic member on weekends (e.g., weekend 
packages or group rides).  
3. Run a "Try Membership for a Week" program to let 
casual users experience the benefits of being a 
member.  

Customer Relationship Management  
1. Introduce weekday membership benefits for casual 
riders, such as lower rates for rides during off-peak 
hours (e.g., before 10 a.m. or after 5 p.m.), when they 
upgrade to a cyclistic member.  
2. Expand service coverage or bike availability on 
Wednesdays and Saturdays, as they are the peak days 
for members and casual users.
